Enter the SCU on each row. Each row is filled with the biggest checked container size that fits its position first, then the next size down, until every SCU is placed. Uncheck a size in the header to skip it.

SCU by position

Containers lie flat, with their longest side level, and can turn to point fore–aft or port–starboard. Every container sits on the floor or fully on other containers. The packing is a quick estimate, so a tight load it says won't fit may still fit by hand.
